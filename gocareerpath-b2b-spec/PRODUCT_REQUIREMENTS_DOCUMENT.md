# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Introduction & Purpose

This document outlines the product requirements for the GoCareerPath B2B Upskilling & Internal Mobility Intelligence Platform. The primary goal of this product is to provide managers and organizational leaders with actionable insights into their teams' skills, enabling them to make data-driven decisions regarding training, promotions, and talent management.

## 2. Target Audience

The primary users of this platform are:

*   **Managers:** Team leads and department heads who are responsible for the growth and development of their direct reports.
*   **Organizational Leaders:** VPs, Directors, and HR leaders who are responsible for the overall talent strategy and organizational health.

## 3. Product Vision & Strategy

The vision is to create a minimal-friction, high-impact platform that leverages existing data (GoCareerPath consumer reports) to provide immediate value to enterprises. The strategy is to focus on a manager-centric MVP that delivers core insights around skill gaps, promotion readiness, and training ROI.

## 4. Core Business Requirements

The platform must enable managers and org leaders to:

*   **View Employee "Skill Snapshots":**
    *   Key strengths, gaps, and progress on career roadmaps.
    *   Time-to-mastery estimates for key skills.
    *   Automation-risk scores.
    *   Promotion timeline predictions.
    *   Projected productivity uplift from skill development.
*   **Compare Talent Across Teams:**
    *   Identify employees who are ready for promotion.
    *   Flag individuals at high risk of attrition.
    *   Spot teams with significant or widening skill gaps.
*   **Quantify Training ROI:**
    *   Track skill improvements over time (deltas).
    *   Correlate skill uplift with training investments.
    *   Attribute improvements to specific learning activities.
*   **Generate Team-Level Intelligence:**
    *   Visualize skill heatmaps across different roles.
    *   Assess team readiness for upcoming projects.
    *   Analyze replacement risk for critical roles.

## 5. Key Features & Functionality

### 5.1. Manager Dashboard

A centralized view for managers to see an overview of their team's skill landscape.

*   **Team Skill Heatmap:** A visual representation of skills across the team.
*   **Promotion Readiness Index:** A list of employees who are ready for promotion, based on their skill profile.
*   **Attrition Risk Index:** A list of high-value employees who are at risk of leaving.
*   **Key Skill Gaps:** A summary of the most critical skill gaps within the team.

### 5.2. Employee Skill Snapshot

A detailed view of an individual employee's skill profile.

*   **Current Skills:** A list of the employee's current skills, including proficiency levels.
*   **Skill Gaps:** A list of skills that need development for their current or future roles.
*   **Career Roadmap:** A personalized career path with recommended roles and skills.
*   **Projections:** AI-driven predictions for promotion timeline, productivity uplift, and automation risk.

### 5.3. Training ROI Panel

A dedicated section to track the impact of training initiatives.

*   **Skill Deltas:** Before-and-after comparisons of skill profiles.
*   **Investment Tracking:** The ability to log training investments (e.g., course fees, time spent).
*   **ROI Calculation:** An automated calculation of the return on training investment.

### 5.4. Data Import & Onboarding

A simple process for companies to get started.

*   **Org Chart Upload:** The ability to upload a CSV or integrate with an HRIS to import the company's organizational chart.
*   **Report Mapping:** A mechanism to map employees to their existing GoCareerPath consumer reports.

## 6. MVP Constraints

*   **Manager-Focused:** The MVP will be designed exclusively for managers. There will be no employee-facing features.
*   **Existing Data Only:** All insights will be derived from existing GoCareerPath consumer reports. No new data collection from employees will be required.
*   **Minimal Friction:** The onboarding process should be as simple as possible, requiring only an org chart and report mapping.

## 7. Key Outcomes for Enterprise Users

*   Faster, more accurate promotions.
*   Lower attrition rates for high-value employees.
*   Higher productivity and team performance.
*   Reduced recruiting costs through internal mobility.
*   Clear visibility into team capabilities and skill gaps.
*   Evidence-based decisions for training and development.
*   A stronger bench of talent for senior and critical roles.
*   Data to justify and optimize training budgets.
